## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of mindful Korean gift-giving, where seasonal local produce meets contemporary artisan pastry craftsmanship. It channels the calm, contemplative warmth of a modern hanok tea house intertwined with the tactile sophistication of premium confection packaging.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Atmosphere
- **Mindful & Heartfelt (온기):** Evokes the care of receiving a curated monthly seasonal parcel wrapped in delicate bojagi fabric and handmade paper.
- **Rooted & Artful (단아함):** Celebrates native terroir (local chestnuts, seasonal persimmons, mugwort, and Jeju matcha) with disciplined minimalism.
- **Sensory & Trustworthy (신뢰):** Balancing clean culinary transparency with tactile luxury, assuring customers of impeccable ingredient provenance and gifting etiquette.

### Design Movement
The system merges **Warm Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage Luxury**. Crisp editorial typographic structures meet soft, butter-creamy backgrounds, delicate hairline boundaries inspired by Korean paper (hanji) frames, and gentle, pillowed depth that honors packaging textures rather than clinical digital flatness.

## Colors

The palette reflects authentic culinary elements: roasted grain, dark walnut woodwork, stone-ground green tea, and slow-baked pastry crusts set over a warm, unbleached ivory foundation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3E2723` / Deep Walnut):** Serves as the primary anchor for core typography, authoritative headers, high-tier CTAs, and structural accents. Replaces harsh blacks with rich roasted depth.
- **Secondary (`#5A7850` / Matcha Sage):** Signifies seasonal freshness, farm provenance tags, freshness badges, and natural agricultural origins.
- **Tertiary (`#D9822B` / Bakery Amber):** Represents caramelization, golden gift ribbon accents, seasonal highlights, and warmth cues.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` / Butter Cream Ivory):** The canvas base, establishing a soft visual atmosphere that reduces eye strain and provides natural contrast for packaging photography.

### Surface Color Logic
- **Background Base:** `#FDFBF7` (Canvas base)
- **Container Elevated:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure card surface with subtle warmth)
- **Container Recessed:** `#F6F1EA` (Tonal distinction for secondary product specs and background chips)
- **Dividers & Strokes:** `#E7DFD5` (Soft, non-aggressive boundaries reminiscent of traditional wood joinery lines)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a harmonious balance between refined editorial lyricism and utilitarian Korean mobile legibility.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Serif Display Titles (`Noto Serif`):** Reserved for evocative storytelling, monthly curated fruit introductions, artisanal craftsmanship notes, and gift packaging chapter headings. Gives the brand its literary, tranquil character.
- **Sans-Serif Functional Structure (`Noto Sans`):** Anchors all interactive components, nutritional/origin ingredient details, pricing, filter chips, and primary actions. Ensures crisp readability on mobile displays across varied viewports.
- **Vertical Rhythm & Tracking:** Korean glyphs require generous line-heights (1.5–1.7x body font size) to breathe comfortably, paired with slight negative tracking on display sizes to prevent visual fragmentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces mobile-first vertical pacing with generous breathing room, avoiding crowded portal-style layouts while retaining clear discovery pathways.

### Rhythm & Fluid Structure
- **Mobile Container:** Optimized for single-column continuous storytelling. The content column respects an outer margin of `1.25rem` (`20px`) on standard smartphones, expanding to `1.5rem` (`24px`) on tablets.
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid 4-column system for small screens; elements span 2 or 4 columns with a fixed `1rem` (`16px`) gutter.
- **Visual Cadence:** Content blocks transition through intentional vertical voids (`space-xl` / `40px`) to evoke the calm pacing of an exhibition catalog.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft tonal layering and warm, diffuse ambient drop shadows rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#FDFBF7` unbleached paper surface.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Product Modules):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with an ultra-soft amber-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.05)` and a micro-border of `1px solid #E7DFD5`. This mimics high-grade textured cardstock resting on a wooden table.
3. **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Sticky Navigation):** Frosted glass container (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a subtle bottom divider, ensuring content rolls gently behind navigation bars without harsh breaks.
4. **Level 3 (Gift Modals & Drawers):** `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(62, 39, 35, 0.12)`, grounding modal interactions with warm walnut undertones.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a rounded posture (`roundedness: 2`, `8px` baseline radius), mirroring traditional Korean confections, smooth river stones, and curved pottery edges.

### Application Rules
- **Base Components (Buttons, Input Fields, Standard Chips):** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for a tailored, welcoming feel.
- **Gift Set & Ingredient Highlight Cards:** `1rem` (`16px`) corner radius to emulate boxed packaging and cloth-tied hampers.
- **Pills & Seasonal Badges:** Full circular pill radii (`9999px`) reserved strictly for micro-status indicators (e.g., "10월 한정", "로컬 백자 과일").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Walnut Solid):** Background `#3E2723`, text `#FDFBF7`, padding `14px 24px`, radius `8px`. Micro-feedback includes an opacity ease to `0.92` with zero harsh translation.
- **Secondary (Matcha Line):** Outlined with `1.5px solid #5A7850`, background transparent, text `#5A7850`. Active state introduces subtle tinted fill (`#E8EFE5`).
- **Tertiary/Ghost (Warm Neutral):** Text `#3E2723`, background `#F6F1EA`, padding `10px 18px`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Filter Chip:** Neutral base `#F6F1EA`, text `#5D4037`, radius `9999px`, font size `13px`. Selected state turns `#3E2723` with white text and a clean checkmark glyph.
- **Seasonal Provenance Badge:** Subtle matcha wash `#E8EFE5`, border `1px solid rgba(90, 120, 80, 0.25)`, text `#5A7850`, font size `11px`, bold and uppercase letter tracking.

### Cards (Gift & Dessert Modules)
- **Product Presentation Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded `16px`, border `1px solid #E7DFD5`. Features an aspect-ratio-locked image frame (`4:5` for editorial dessert photography), followed by serif title, regional origin metadata tag, and bakery amber price highlight.
- **Editorial Seasonal Story Card:** Recessed `#F6F1EA` container, subtle vertical typography embellishment, highlighting farm harvest dates and producer signatures.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Inputs:** Border `1px solid #E7DFD5`, background `#FFFFFF`, inner padding `14px 16px`, corner radius `8px`, placeholder color `#8A7D75`. Focused state illuminates the outline with `#3E2723` at `1.5px` without jarring glow rings.
- **Selection Controls (Radios & Checkboxes):** Custom rounded indicators with an active walnut fill (`#3E2723`) and ivory check icon.

### Bespoke Brand Components
- **Bojagi Ribbon Divider:** A subtle graphic ornament consisting of two refined horizontal rules converging on an artisan knot glyph, signaling chapter shifts in storytelling.
- **Seasonal Origin Tag:** A tactile pill component highlighting monthly regional harvests (e.g., `공주 햇밤 · 10월 한정`) placed at the top-left of hero dessert imagery.
